TauREx4: eScience grant for the next generation of our retrieval code

Quentin Changeat
Nov 17, 2026 1 min read
Image Credit: TauREx team

Our group has been awarded a Netherlands eScience Center grant to build TauREx4, the next generation of the TauREx atmospheric retrieval framework.

Over the coming decade, JWST, ESO's Extremely Large Telescope and ESA's Ariel mission will deliver an unprecedented volume of exoplanet atmosphere observations. Interpreting these data means exploring very large and complex parameter spaces, at a scale that current atmospheric retrieval tools struggle to handle.

With TauREx4 — Atmospheric Retrievals for Modern Exoplanet Data, funded through the eScience Center's 2025 Open eScience Call (grant NLESC.OEC.2025.146), we will redesign TauREx, one of the most widely used retrieval codes in the field. The project modernises its computational and statistical core and brings in machine-learning techniques, with the aim of setting new standards for inverse problems in exoplanet science.

The project runs until 2029 and is led by University of Groningen, eScience Center, SRON, University College London, and King's College London SRON.
