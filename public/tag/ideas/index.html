Coupling Magma─Ocean and Atmospheres in Spectral Retrievals of Sub-Neptunes
The study couples interior and atmosphere models to study sub-Neptune hosting a deep lava ocean. The model is embeded in our TauREx3 retrieval framework, allowing to directly retrieve the composition of the magma ocean from its impact on the observable atmospheric composition.
Yuichi Ito, Quentin Changeat
